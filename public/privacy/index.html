Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Digital Kids Foundation Inc. (DKF) is a New York nonprofit organization. This policy explains how we handle information through our website, online forms, donation services, and DKF mobile applications when available.

Information you provide

When you contact DKF, apply to volunteer, offer a device donation, submit a laptop assistance application, or communicate with our team, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, location, organization details, and the information requested by the applicable form.

A parent, guardian, or authorized representative applying on behalf of a child may provide limited information about the child that is relevant to the request. Please do not enter a child’s name in the age field or other fields unless DKF specifically requests it.

Technical, security, and usage information

Our hosting, security, and anti-bot services may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and security signals. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to help prevent spam and automated abuse.

Analytics and advertising measurement

DKF uses Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags, Google Analytics 4 to understand website use, and Google Ads measurement to evaluate whether advertising leads to meaningful actions such as completed laptop-donation or partnership inquiries.

These tools may use cookies, browser storage, IP-derived information, device or browser identifiers, page URLs, referral information, and interaction events. DKF does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, form contents, or information about children to Google Analytics or Google Ads.

Page views may be measured for reporting, while successful inquiry events are used to understand campaign performance. Where applicable law requires consent, DKF will use appropriate consent controls before enabling optional analytics or advertising storage.

Cookies and your choices

You can limit or delete cookies and similar storage through your browser settings. Blocking these technologies may affect certain website features or measurement. Where a consent choice is presented, you may accept or decline optional measurement. You may also use Google’s available browser and account controls for advertising and analytics preferences.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, review laptop assistance applications, coordinate volunteering and device donations, verify email addresses where required, communicate about programs, prevent spam and abuse, maintain security, process or verify donations, and keep necessary administrative records.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use children’s application information for targeted advertising.

Laptop assistance applications and children

Children cannot independently submit laptop assistance applications. An adult parent, guardian, or authorized representative must complete the application. Information submitted about a child is used to review the request, communicate with the adult applicant, and coordinate assistance if resources become available.

Donations and payment providers

PayPal or another payment provider may collect payment and account information under its own privacy policy. DKF does not ask you to enter payment-card information into its contact, volunteer, device-donation, or laptop-assistance forms.

DKF may receive transaction identifiers, donation amounts, currency, and payment status from the payment provider so that completed donations can be verified, recorded, acknowledged, and measured without sending payment-card details to DKF.

Service providers

DKF may use service providers for hosting, database services, email delivery, security, anti-bot protection, analytics, advertising measurement, and payment processing. Providers process information only as needed to perform their services and under their applicable terms.

Sharing

We may share information with authorized DKF personnel and volunteers who need it to administer programs, with service providers supporting our operations, or when required by law or necessary to protect safety. We do not publish form submissions as testimonials or donor acknowledgments without appropriate permission.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, operational needs, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may contact DKF to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and operational exceptions. You may decline optional information, although some information is needed to process an inquiry or application.

To make a privacy request, use the website contact form or visit the Data Deletion page.

Children’s privacy

Our website and mobile apps are intended primarily for parents, guardians, donors, partners, and prospective volunteers. Children should not submit forms or personal information independently. If you believe a child submitted information without appropriate adult involvement, contact DKF so we can review the situation.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as our website, mobile apps, programs, providers, or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page.

Questions? Contact DKF through the website Contact page.
