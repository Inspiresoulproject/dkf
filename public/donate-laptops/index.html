Our purpose

Every child deserves access to technology

We envision a future where children and families have meaningful opportunities to participate in an increasingly digital world, regardless of economic circumstances. Through technology donations, community partnerships, and digital skills initiatives, we aim to create lasting opportunities.

How we work together

How to donate laptops and other devices

Have a working laptop, tablet, or Chromebook you no longer need? Individuals and businesses can contact us about donating devices to help children in need access technology.

Tell us what you have

We especially need laptops, Chromebooks, and tablets. Tell us how many you have, their condition, and whether chargers are included.

We’ll discuss drop-off or pickup

We’ll review your donation and discuss how to receive it. Pickup may be possible depending on your location, the number of devices, and availability.

Protect your information

Please back up your files and sign out of your accounts. Ask us about secure data wiping options for eligible devices.

Current initiative

Help Bridge the Digital Divide in Brooklyn

Every child deserves access to technology. Through our Brooklyn Digital Access Initiative, Digital Kids Foundation is working to collect 40–50 laptops to expand access to technology for children in need and underserved communities.
