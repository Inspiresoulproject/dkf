Data Deletion Requests

Digital Kids Foundation (DKF) respects your privacy. This page explains how to request deletion of personal information submitted through the Digital Kids Foundation website and Android or iOS mobile applications.

No account is required. The DKF app does not provide user accounts. You can request deletion of information you submitted through its forms.

How to request deletion

Send an email to report@digitalkidsfoundation.org with the subject Data Deletion Request.

In your email, please tell us which DKF form or service you used (for example, laptop assistance, volunteering, device donation, or contact form), the email address you used when submitting it, and which information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive identification documents.

For laptop assistance applications involving a child, the request should come from the parent or legal guardian who submitted the application.

What information can be deleted

Depending on your submission, this may include your name, email address, phone number, location or address, application responses, volunteer interests, donation details, and a child's age and grade level provided by a parent or guardian.

We will review the request, identify information associated with your submission, and delete eligible personal information from systems under our control.

Information we may need to retain

Some records may need to be kept for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, security, or legitimate program administration reasons. If information cannot be deleted immediately, we will explain what is retained and why, where appropriate. Any retention period depends on the applicable requirement; DKF does not promise a fixed deletion period for all records.

Information held independently by third-party services, such as payment processors, may be subject to their own retention and deletion procedures.

How requests are handled

We may ask for limited information to verify that you are the person who submitted the information, or the relevant parent or legal guardian. We will review your request and respond with the outcome, including any information that must be retained. Please use the email address associated with your original submission when possible.

Questions and privacy policy

For questions about data deletion or DKF's privacy practices, contact report@digitalkidsfoundation.org. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
