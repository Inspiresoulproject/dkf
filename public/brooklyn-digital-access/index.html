Why this work matters

Every child should be able to learn at home.

A laptop can help a student finish an assignment, explore a new idea, or practice a digital skill that matters later in life. Yet not every household has access to a computer that can keep up with those needs.

Digital Kids Foundation Inc. is developing the Brooklyn Digital Access Initiative to help children facing financial barriers gain meaningful access to technology—through thoughtfully donated devices and relationships with people who know the community.

40–50

Our first goal: 40–50 laptops

Our first goal is to collect 40–50 usable laptops for children in Brooklyn. We also welcome inquiries about Chromebooks and tablets. We will work with local partners to prepare and share suitable devices when the program is ready.

This is a fundraising and equipment acquisition goal, not a count of delivered laptops.

How we plan to work

How we plan to prepare donated devices

We want every donated device to be useful, secure, and placed where it can make a real difference. Our planned process has four practical steps.

01

Welcome donations

Ask individuals and businesses to donate usable laptops, Chromebooks, tablets, and suitable chargers.

02

Prepare with care

Inventory devices, verify secure data erasure, and test or refurbish equipment as needed.

03

Work locally

Connect with Brooklyn community organizations to share program information with families.

04

Distribute fairly

Review applications against clear criteria, document handovers, and offer basic device guidance.

Who the initiative is for

Designed for children who need access most.

The first pilot is intended for children in Brooklyn whose families face barriers to purchasing a suitable computer. As the program develops, DKF plans to use consistent, needs-based eligibility criteria.

  • Children who lack a reliable computer for schoolwork
  • Households facing financial barriers to technology
  • Applications completed with parent or guardian involvement
  • Fair review and careful handling of personal information

Information for Brooklyn families

Applications are not open for this initiative yet. We will announce eligibility, timelines, and next steps once enough equipment and partner arrangements are in place. We cannot promise a laptop before devices are secured and ready.

Frequently asked questions

Need more detail? Reach our team using the contact form.

Are 40–50 laptops already available?

Not yet. This is our initial goal. DKF is currently seeking laptop donors and community partners.

Can my company donate retired laptops?

We welcome inquiries from financial institutions, technology companies, and other businesses about surplus equipment. Donors should follow their own approved IT asset handling and data sanitization procedures before transfer.

What if I'm interested in a community partnership?

Please use the Contact Us form and choose "Corporate / School Partnership". We welcome conversations with organizations already serving Brooklyn children and families.

When can families apply?

The program is being prepared and applications are not open yet. We will post official announcements when the initiative is ready.
