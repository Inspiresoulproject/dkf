Who We Are

DKF is a Brooklyn-based, IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to bridge the digital divide by helping children access the technology they need to learn and thrive.

Our Foundation

Purpose-driven from day one

🎯

Mission

Help children in need and underserved children access technology by providing refurbished laptops, tablets, Chromebooks, and digital resources — at no cost to families.

🔭

Vision

A society where all children, regardless of their background or circumstances, have access to the technology they need to unlock their full potential and thrive in a digital world.

🏆

Goals

Collect and refurbish used laptops, tablets, and digital tools for children in need who lack access to technology, while reducing e-waste and building digital literacy across communities.

Our Story

Why we started — and why it still matters

Digital Kids Foundation began its work in 2024 with a belief that every child deserves access to technology and the opportunity to learn. These early laptop donations took place through a predecessor community project before Digital Kids Foundation Inc. was formally incorporated as a New York nonprofit on October 3, 2025.

During 2024–2025, a predecessor community project distributed 12 laptops to children in Ghana and Brooklyn, New York. With the generous support of NYC Seminar and Conference Center, 10 refurbished Dell laptops were donated to underserved children in Ghana. The predecessor community project also donated two laptops to children in Brooklyn.

These early efforts inspire our continued mission: to connect underserved children with computers, digital literacy, and technology opportunities.

The Digital Divide is Real

Many children in underserved communities face barriers to reliable computer access at home. We aim to help close that gap.

♻️

E-Waste is a Crisis Too

Electronic waste is one of the world’s fastest-growing waste streams. Refurbishing extends device life and helps keep harmful materials out of landfills.

📈

More Digital Opportunities

Access to a reliable computer can open doors to digital skills, information, creativity, and connection.

Who We Serve

Every child deserves a fair start

We seek to work with community organizations to reach children who face the biggest barriers to technology access.

Children Without Reliable Devices

We aim to help children in underserved communities who face barriers to accessing a reliable device.

Foster Youth

Children in foster care may face changes in access to technology. A dependable device can help them stay connected. A personal device can be a constant — something that's theirs, wherever they go.

Immigrant Families

Newly arrived families often lack resources to purchase technology. We hope to connect with community organizations that support families with limited access to devices.

Community Centers

We hope to work with trusted community groups to help children who have limited access to technology.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good questions make great beginnings.

Explore how Digital Kids Foundation is getting started, how you can help, and what to expect as our programs grow.

01

About DKF

Digital Kids Foundation is a New York nonprofit working to expand access to laptops, tablets, Chromebooks, and technology for children in need and underserved communities.
We are developing our current nonprofit programs. A predecessor community project distributed 12 laptops during 2024–2025, before Digital Kids Foundation Inc. was incorporated. These are historical predecessor-project results, not distributions by the incorporated nonprofit. We will share verified milestones for current programs as they occur.
Our organization is based in New York. Our mission focuses on children in need and underserved communities through device access and community partnerships.
Yes. Digital Kids Foundation Inc. is a New York nonprofit corporation based in Brooklyn and is officially recognized by the IRS as a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3). Our IRS determination letter is available to donors and partners upon request.
02

Device Donations

Yes. We welcome inquiries about donating usable laptops and related accessories. Please contact us with the device model, condition, and approximate age so we can confirm suitability and arrange next steps.
Working laptops and Chromebooks with compatible chargers are especially useful for our mission. For tablets, desktops, damaged devices, or larger batches, please ask before arranging a donation.
Back up your files, sign out of your accounts, and securely erase personal information before donating. Contact us if you need guidance on data removal.
Device pickup may be arranged case by case, depending on location, quantity, and availability. Contact our team before sending or transporting equipment.
03

Giving & Transparency

Please use our donation or contact page to ask about current ways to support the foundation. Availability of payment options may change as our fundraising systems are established.
Potential expenses include laptop repairs, replacement parts, logistics, digital literacy materials, and program operations. Specific costs vary; we do not publish fixed per-device impact amounts without verified program data.
Eligible donations to Digital Kids Foundation Inc. are generally tax-deductible to the extent permitted by U.S. law. We provide official donation acknowledgments for accepted contributions. Donors should consult their tax adviser regarding their specific circumstances.
We plan to publish transparent updates as donations, refurbishments, distributions, and workshops take place. We will distinguish planned activities from completed results.
04

Volunteering & Partnerships

Visit our Volunteer page to express interest. Potential opportunities include device assessment, repairs, digital skills mentoring, community outreach, and administrative support. Roles depend on current program needs.
Yes. We welcome conversations with businesses, nonprofits, and community groups about device donations, volunteer support, and technology access.
We aim to use needs-based eligibility and a fair review process when distribution begins. Specific application and selection procedures will be shared before the first distribution.
Use our Contact page to send a message. Our team can help with donation inquiries, volunteering, partnerships, and organizational questions.
