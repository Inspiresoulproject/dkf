# DKF Typography and Thank You Screen Polish

## What changed

- Reduced the vertical spacing of the Volunteer and Device Donation confirmation screens.
- Preserved the centered checkmark, Thank You heading, confirmation text, and Back to Home Page button.
- Increased small supporting text across the main website from roughly 10–13px into a more readable 12–16px range.
- Increased form labels, notes, checkbox text, FAQ answers, cards, footer links, and mobile navigation labels.
- Increased small text on Donate Laptops, Corporate Partnerships, and Brooklyn Digital Access landing pages.
- Increased popup-form labels and inputs; inputs remain 16px to prevent unwanted iPhone zoom.
- Increased public laptop-assistance form text and donation-success supporting text.

## Typography approach

- Major headings were kept at their existing sizes.
- Body copy remains approximately 16px or larger.
- Supporting text is generally 14–16px.
- Eyebrows and image captions remain smaller by design, but no primary website text is below 12px in the reviewed mobile layout.
- The compact mobile footer and bottom navigation remain compact.

## Preserved

- Contact Us mobile layout
- Compact mobile footer
- Mobile header logo
- Cloudflare Turnstile
- Forms and backend validation
- GTM, GA4, and Google Ads events
- PayPal verification infrastructure
- Existing colors, images, and brand fonts
