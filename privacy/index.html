Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

Who we are

Digital Kids Foundation (DKF) is a New York nonprofit organization. This policy describes how we handle information through our website, related online forms, and Digital Kids Foundation mobile applications for Android and iOS when available.

Information we collect

When you contact DKF, apply to volunteer, offer a device donation, or submit a laptop assistance application, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, location, and information entered in the applicable form. A parent or legal guardian applying for laptop assistance on behalf of a child may provide information about that child relevant to the application. Technical logs and security or anti-bot services may process IP addresses, device or browser information, and security-related data.

Laptop assistance applications and children

Children cannot independently submit laptop assistance applications. A parent or legal guardian must complete and submit an application on the child’s behalf. Information submitted about a child is used to review the request, determine program eligibility, communicate with the parent or guardian, and coordinate assistance. Parents or guardians may contact DKF to request access to, correction of, or deletion of submitted information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, review laptop assistance applications, coordinate volunteering and device donations, verify email addresses, communicate about programs, prevent spam and abuse, maintain website and app security, and keep necessary administrative records. We do not sell personal information or use children’s application information for targeted advertising.

Email verification and service providers

Some forms use email verification. DKF uses service providers for hosting, email delivery, security and anti-bot checks, and donation or payment processing where applicable. Providers may process information as needed to provide these services, subject to their applicable privacy terms.

Donations and payments

When you use a third-party donation or payment service, that provider may collect payment details under its own privacy policy. Do not enter payment-card information into DKF application, contact, or volunteer forms.

Sharing

We do not publish form submissions as testimonials or donor acknowledgments without appropriate permission. We may share information with authorized DKF personnel and volunteers who need it to administer programs, with service providers supporting our operations, or when required by law or necessary to protect safety.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, operational needs, and applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

Our website and mobile apps are intended primarily for parents, guardians, donors, and prospective volunteers, rather than for children to submit information independently. Children should not submit forms or personal information directly. If you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate parental involvement, please contact DKF.

Mobile app information

The Digital Kids Foundation mobile app provides access to DKF programs, applications, volunteer opportunities, and donation features. Information entered in app forms is handled for the same purposes described in this policy. The app may communicate with DKF services and third-party providers to operate these features. Device permissions, if requested, should be reviewed in the app and operating-system settings.

Your choices

You may contact DKF through the website contact form to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational exceptions. You may decline optional requests for information; some services may require information to process an application or inquiry.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as our website, mobile apps, programs, or practices change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page.
